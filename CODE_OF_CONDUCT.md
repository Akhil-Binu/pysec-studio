# Community Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

We are dedicated to providing a welcoming, inclusive, and harassment-free environment for all contributors and participants in the PySecStudio project, regardless of background, identity, or experience level.

## Standards & Expectations

To maintain a healthy and productive open-source community:

- **Be Respectful**: Value diverse viewpoints, constructive feedback, and differing levels of experience.
- **Collaborate with Empathy**: Communicate professionally and courteously in all project discussions, issue comments, and review threads.
- **Focus on What Is Best for the Community**: Prioritize security, quality, and the collaborative success of the project.
- **Unacceptable Behavior**: Harassment, derogatory comments, personal attacks, trolling, or publishing private information without explicit consent are not tolerated.

## Reporting & Enforcement

If you witness or experience unacceptable behavior, please report it to the repository maintainers. All concerns will be reviewed promptly and handled with discretion.
